# motor assemblies

You can use any `motor_assembly` to power any `feeder`, including your own motor assembly that is not listed below.

* We have successfully used DC brushed and brushless motors, AC motors, and stepper motors.
* In general, you want something that can provide a relatively large amount of torque at low RPM.
* In general, you do not need a motor with a top end of more than 100 RPM.

There are many thousands of different motors available on the internet across sites like Amazon, Alibaba, DFRobot, Adafruit, etc. 

## Described assemblies

**manual_control_ac_motor**

You can buy a complete motor assembly that includes a power cord, control knob, and motor mount, and the motor itself. 
For example, [this AC motor from Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07WH5V14Z). 

This is probably the easiest motor assembly to "build" to run one of the feeders, because there is essentially zero assembly required.
